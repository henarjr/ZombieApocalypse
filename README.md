# Zombie Apocalypse 🧟

**Zombie Apocalypse** is a small 2D survival game built with Unity, where your goal is simple: survive for as long as you can.

Explore different environments, from abandoned urban areas to frozen lands and scorching deserts. Collect food, encounter zombies, and make your way through a world where survival is never guaranteed.

Sounds easy... until the zombies show up. 💀

## 🎮 Gameplay

Explore the map, manage your survival, and face the zombies along the way.

Move through different environments, collect food to stay alive, and decide when to fight or avoid enemies.

Your goal? Survive the apocalypse for as long as possible!

## 🕹️ How to Play

| **Input** | **Action** |
|---|---|
| `Arrow Keys` | Move |
| Walk into zombies | Fight them! |

## ✨ Features

- 2D roguelike-inspired survival gameplay.
- Different environments to explore.
- Player movement and enemy interactions.
- Food collection and survival mechanics.
- Zombie encounters and combat.
- Turn-based gameplay.

## 🛠️ My Work & Learning

This project was developed while following Unity Learn's **2D Roguelike Tutorial**.

I completed the tutorial challenges and used the project as an opportunity to practice and understand different aspects of 2D game development.

Throughout the process, I worked with:
- Player movement and game board systems.
- Turn-based gameplay mechanics.
- Enemy behavior and interactions.
- Food resources and survival systems.
- Sprites, animations, and game assets provided by the tutorial.

This project helped me gain more practical experience with Unity, understand how different gameplay systems work together, and become more comfortable building 2D games.
